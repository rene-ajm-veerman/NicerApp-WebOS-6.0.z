What forms of telepathy are there?

Sub-vocal thoughts

You can be given an idea by telepathy, without it being transferred to you as speech audio data, or as an image. The idea sent to you will "unfold" in your own brain by using your own vocabulary and your own intonation to vocalize the idea. This can be just as much as a whisper in your brain, which could also be called the "intuition" level.


This means it can be very hard (at first, but still so as an experienced telepath) to detect that these messages are in fact telepathy sent to you.

Sounds and speech

In this form of telepathy, you'll actually hear a voice inside your head, maybe as quiet as a whisper, maybe loud enough to drown out what you hear in your normal ears on the sides of your head, but you'll (usually?) know that this sound is not coming from your own ears. If you can not distinguish between what is audio-telepathy and what are your own ears (so that you can not tell if the sounds are coming from near around you or farther away or from dimensions that are as yet unseen by human science), then you're best off seeking professional help as you suffer from auditory hallucinations.

If you're lucky enough to know that the voices are not coming from your own ears, then you're left with a few important choices;

  • Whether or not to believe what you're told through telepathy

  • Whether or not to "speak" back, which is as simple as answering the thought or voice with your own thoughts. That's right, thinking back is enough to reply with your own telepathy to those that invoked your thoughts. That can make it kind of hard to stay out of a discussion that you're effectively drawn or forced into.

  • What side to pick in any dispute between groups of beings all sharing the same telepathic audio channel (think of it like a chat channel)

  • If you for some reason get involved or invested in the discussion, how much of a leadership role will you take on? With more leadership comes more risk, and (sometimes?) responsibility, usually.



Pictures and video

Sometimes a picture sent to you via telepathy is very like a sketch and blinks for less than a second in your "mind's eye" (your visual cortex in your brain), but if you got your eyes closed in a dark room, you might see pictures and even video sent to you by telepathy in your mind's eye that are as clear as when you'd have your eyes open.

I am of the opinion that if you accept this imagery when offered (whose doing that we'll get into in the Angels section), you expose yourself to risk of being sent very unpleasant imagery that you can not resist later on, because you accepted pleasant imagery for so long. So before accepting imagery, you have to resist it plenty of times. You can resist that imagery by lying in bed or sitting in a comfortable chair or even standing upright and mostly still, then closing your eyes, if necessary wearing a blindfold around your eyes with just enough pressure to keep your eyelids closed, and focussing your mind's attention not only on keeping your body relaxed and your breathing relaxed, but also on the signal you get from your now nice-and-dark optical nerves; the 2 nerves, signal paths to your brain, that run all the way from your eyeballs to your visual cortex. Whenever imagery you're sent, even pleasant or interesting or exciting imagery, you block out by focussing on your optical nerve. It is my opinion that only then can you start to accept imagery from outside sources (which are possibly invisible to human science), because you can now block out unpleasant imagery as soon as it's sent to you.


Whatever you do, do not use this imagery to fall asleep with time and time again. That makes you completely dependent on the ones who are providing this imagery to you, and could very well make you an indentured servant of them without your knowledge or permission.
