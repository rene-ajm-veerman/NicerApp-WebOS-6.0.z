The mathematics of Karma

Karma technicalities are simple, value-systems are Veeeery complicated... I'll have to do more drawings..
Anyways; Take a 3D cross, with the following quadrants:
  • Top-right quadrant is Positive Action
  • Bottom-right quadrant is Negative Action
  • Bottom-Left quadrant is Negative Communication
  • Top-Left quadrant is Positive Communication
  • The vertical axis runs from -100 to +100 %
    On this vertical axis, each side, you place your 'values'. I will be making mine shortly, but not today ;)
  • The horizontal axis runs from -10 000 to +10 000 affected souls.

I propose we measure Karma for humans per Week or per Month. NOT PER DAY. ;-)
Examples:
  • I run a newspaper (editor job) that is _really_ truthfull and gets read by 1000 people, but this week i killed someone's dog in a carcrash.
    I had the cash but not the guts/time to replace the dog, or even to talk to the owners (a family of 4ppl) of the dog.
    The 2 kids who loved the dog count double, so _karmatically_, 6 souls were affected. This gives me a Karma of
    • Newspaper editor: Action50 * 1000 = 50000
    • Killing Dog: Action -50 * 6 = -300
    • Balance : 49700+
    As you can see, this is just the beginning of this form of accountancy... I need to do more research...
